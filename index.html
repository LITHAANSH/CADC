Answers to Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent information on our consultancy delivery timelines, course structures, and technical standards.

Which CATIA versions do you support for consultancy and courses?

+
We support all industry-standard releases, including CATIA V5-6R (R2018 through R2024) as well as Dassault Systèmes 3DEXPERIENCE platform (Cloud & On-Premise). Deliverables can be tailored to match your company's exact release level.

How quickly can you start on an urgent design project?

+
For critical aerospace or automotive projects, our senior engineers can review requirements, sign bilateral NDAs, and begin active CAD execution within 24 to 48 hours of project kickoff.

Are the courses self-paced or instructor-led?

+
Our courses feature a hybrid modern model: comprehensive step-by-step modular project video walkthroughs paired with live milestone reviews and 1-on-1 feedback sessions with practicing senior engineers.

Do students receive certification and project deliverables?

+
Yes. Every course graduate receives a verified CADC Academy Certificate of Completion along with a production-grade portfolio project containing complete native 3D CATParts, CATProducts, and GD&T drawing sheets.

Do you provide on-site or customized corporate team courses?

+
Yes. We regularly deliver customized multi-week training programs for automotive OEMs, defense contractors, and aerospace design departments, tailored directly to your proprietary product lines and CAD standards.

How do we arrange a touchpoint call to discuss specifications?
