Track 01
Track 01
● Core Foundations

CATIA V5 & 3DEXPERIENCE Part Design Fundamentals

Target: Mechanical, aerospace, and automotive engineers seeking robust CAD foundations.

Master the fundamental solid modeling workbenches in CATIA. Learn proper sketch constraint hygiene (zero under-constrained sketches), boolean solid tree management, draft angles, dress-up features, and associative 2D manufacturing drawings compliant with ASME Y14.5.

Core Curriculum & Milestones:
  • Fully-constrained 2D sketcher mechanics and isostatic constraint rules
  • Pads, Pockets, Shafts, Grooves, Ribs, Multi-sections & Boolean operations
  • 2D Drafting: Auxiliary views, datum reference frames, GD&T callouts & BOM

Track 02
Track 02
● Intermediate / Advanced

Advanced Generative Shape Design (GSD) & Surfacing

Target: Engineers designing aerodynamic fairings, styling surfaces, and sheet metal forms.

Covers 3D wireframe generation and complex multi-section surface creation. Master G0 (point), G1 (tangency), and G2 (curvature) continuity controls, sweep topologies with guide rails, spine definitions, surface healing, and draft analysis for tooling split lines.

Core Curriculum & Milestones:
  • 3D Wireframe: 3D Splines, Projections, Intersections, Comb Curvature analysis
  • GSD Workflows: Multi-section surfaces, blends, adaptive sweeps & G2 fillets
  • Solid conversion: Thick surface, close surface, draft angle checks & parting lines

Track 03
Track 03
● Advanced Mechanism

Assembly Design, DMU Kinematics & Motion Simulation

Target: Designers building multi-component mechanical assemblies and linkage mechanisms.

Learn top-down skeleton architectures and master model methodology with publication links. Set up kinematic mechanisms with revolute, prismatic, cylindrical, and screw joints. Execute dynamic clash scans, clearance analysis, and motion sweep volume generation.

Core Curriculum & Milestones:
  • Top-down skeleton modeling & external reference link management
  • DMU Kinematics: Mechanism creation, degrees of freedom & command drives
  • Interference detection: Dynamic clash analysis, clearance graphs & motion sweeps
Enquire for Track 03 →
Track 04
Track 04
● Specialist Domain

Electrical Harness 3D Routing & Formboard Flattening

Target: Aerospace avionics and automotive EV electrical integration engineers.

Master CATIA Electrical Harness Installation (EHI) and Electrical Wire Routing (EWR). Route 3D bundles through structural airframe channels, configure clamp standoffs and branch points, verify minimum bend radii, and flatten harnesses into 1:1 scale manufacturing formboard drawings (EHF).

Core Curriculum & Milestones:
  • 3D bundle segment routing, slack allowances & bend radius rule checking
  • Connector catalog placement, backshells & protective corrugated sleeving
  • Electrical Harness Flattening (EHF) & 1:1 scale manufacturing nailboards
