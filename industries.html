Why CATIA for these sectors?

CATIA (Computer-Aided Three-dimensional Interactive Application) was originally developed for the aerospace industry and is now the standard tool across both aerospace and automotive. Companies like Airbus, Boeing, Dassault, BMW, and Renault all use CATIA as their primary design software — which is why specialist CATIA skills are in high demand in these sectors.

Same expertise. Different applications.

Whether the end product is an aircraft or a vehicle, the CATIA skills are the same — the application context is what changes.

🔩

Parts & Structures

Aircraft structural parts (wing ribs, fuselage frames, brackets) and vehicle structural parts (body frames, chassis components) all use the same CATIA Part Design and Structural tools.

⊞

Complex Assemblies

Both industries require hundreds of parts to be assembled correctly in 3D before anything is built. CATIA's assembly tools are used to model this — and to simulate movement and check for conflicts.

⚡

Electrical Systems

Aircraft and modern vehicles have complex electrical systems routed through their structures. CATIA's electrical tools are used to plan exactly where every wire runs — a service we provide for both sectors.
