Real projects we've designed and trained for — in aerospace and automotive, using CATIA.

Design Project Assemblies & Motion

Aerospace Supplier — Landing Gear Mechanism

What was needed: A complete 3D assembly of a landing gear retraction mechanism, with a simulation showing how it moves. What we did: Built the full assembly in CATIA and used motion simulation (kinematics) to verify the mechanism moved correctly without any parts colliding. Result: Delivered on time, approved by the client's chief engineer first review.

Design Project Composite Materials

Aircraft Manufacturer — Wing Panel in Composite

What was needed: A wing skin panel designed as a composite part — layers of carbon fibre bonded together for strength with minimal weight. What we did: Designed the full layered composite panel in CATIA, defining each layer's angle and material. Result: Ready for manufacturing review.

Extra Support Part Design

Aerospace Engineering Firm — Structural Bracket Package

What was needed: A 12-person engineering team had a deadline coming up and needed extra CATIA hands to complete a set of structural bracket designs. What we did: Stepped in with two CATIA engineers, working inside the client's CATIA environment and following their naming and filing standards. Result: 40 bracket parts delivered in 2 weeks.

Extra Support Electrical Routing

Aerospace Systems Integrator — Wire Harness Routing

What was needed: A wire harness — the bundle of wires carrying electrical signals — needed to be routed through a new fuselage section in CATIA. The client's team was at capacity. What we did: Routed the complete harness through the 3D fuselage model, including clamp positions and connector definitions. Result: All critical clashes resolved before manufacture.

What was needed: 6 new graduate engineers joining an automotive design team needed to learn CATIA before starting on projects. What we did: Ran Program 1 (Getting Started with CATIA) on-site over 5 days. Each engineer completed a real part-and-assembly project.
