CAD Work Package DMU Kinematics & Assemblies

Aerospace Supplier — Landing Gear Retraction Linkage

Technical Scope: Multi-bar linkage assembly for an aircraft main landing gear retraction system. Built 14 kinematic joint definitions (revolute, prismatic, cylindrical) in CATIA DMU Kinematics, simulated stroke deployment, and generated swept motion envelopes. Outcome: Identified and resolved a 3.2mm interference clash with the wheel-well bulkhead prior to physical tooling; approved at CDR first review.

CAD Work Package Aerospace GSD & Surfacing

Airframe OEM — Carbon Composite Wing Skin & Stiffeners

Technical Scope: Modeled a 16-ply carbon fiber composite upper wing skin panel in CATIA GSD. Established aerodynamic loft curves with G2 curvature continuity, integrated co-cured hat-stiffener runouts, and mapped ply drop-off ramp boundaries compliant with AS9100. Outcome: Delivered manufacturing-ready 3D surface geometry, rosette coordinate definitions, and inspection tool surface models.

Design Surge Support Electrical Harness (EHI/EWR)

Systems Integrator — Avionics Bay 3D Wire Harness

Technical Scope: Routing a 120-conductor avionics wire harness through a congested fuselage bay section in CATIA EHI/EWR. Positioned clamp standoffs, verified bend radii against aerospace guidelines, and generated flattened 1:1 scale manufacturing formboards (EHF). Outcome: Eliminated all routing clashes with adjacent hydraulic lines; delivered full nailboard drawing set.
