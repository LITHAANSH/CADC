Engineering Methodology

Clean Specification Trees & 100% Constraint Lock

High-tolerance aerospace and automotive parts require parametric tree hygiene. In CATIA Part Design, we engineer robust solid bodies with clean parent-child relationships, isolated published geometry, and named functional sets. This ensures that downstream revision updates or parametric dimension changes propagate without feature failures.

Our models include comprehensive manufacturing draft angle checks, split line definitions, fillet transitions, and boolean body logic for complex multi-cavity housings and structural fittings.

Deliverables include native .CATPart files, neutral STEP AP242 datasets with PMI annotations, and complete ASME Y14.5 drafting sheets.
