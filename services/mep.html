Engineering Methodology

3D Routing in Congested Envelopes

Modern airframe avionics bays and EV powertrain packaging demand strict harness segregation, bend radius management, and thermal standoff rules. Using CATIA EHI/EWR, we route 3D flexible bundles through complex structural channels, placing clip standoffs, backshells, and protective conduit wraps.

Every harness segment is validated against OEM minimum bend radius criteria (static and dynamic flex points) and dynamic clearance envelopes against adjacent hydraulic, fuel, and moving mechanism lines.

We use CATIA Electrical Harness Flattening (EHF) to convert 3D space routes into 1:1 scale flat formboard (nailboard) manufacturing drawings complete with connector pinout tables, wire lengths, and bundle diameter callouts.
