Milestone Breakdown

Stage-by-Stage Engineering Execution

📋
Stage 01

Technical Brief & Seed Model Setup

We align on customer CAD modeling standards, coordinate systems, seed part templates, and GD&T datum reference frames. Formal mutual NDAs are executed prior to sharing any proprietary CAD geometry or technical specifications.

✏
Stage 02

Parametric 3D Modeling & Loft Architecture

We build parametric solids and multi-section surface lofts with clean specification trees, published geometry, and 100% isostatic sketch constraint lock. Intermediate milestone reviews ensure alignment before detail dressing.

⬛
Stage 03

DMU Kinematics & Packaging Space Checks

For multi-component assemblies, we establish kinematic mechanism joints (revolute, prismatic, cylindrical) and execute dynamic clash detection scans to verify sweep volumes and ensure zero interference in the packaging envelope.

📂
Stage 04

GD&T 2D Release & Complete Handover

We deliver production-ready ASME Y14.5 / ISO 1101 drafting sheets, complete native .CATPart/.CATProduct datasets, neutral STEP AP242 exports, and 3D PDFs with drawing revision change logs.

Data Security

Your designs stay yours.

Before any files are shared with us, we sign a confidentiality agreement (NDA). This is a standard legal document confirming we'll keep everything you share with us private. We use it with every client, every time — no exceptions.

After the project is finished and delivered, we delete your files from our systems. We never use client designs for anything other than the project they were shared for.

  • ⊙
    NDA before any file exchange

    A confidentiality agreement is signed before you share anything with us. We accept client-provided NDAs too.

  • ⊙
    Files deleted after delivery

    All your design files are deleted from our systems within 30 days of final delivery.

  • ⊙
    Secure file transfer

    We can receive and share files through your preferred secure method — encrypted email, cloud storage, or your company's own file system.
