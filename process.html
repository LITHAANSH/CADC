Step by Step

What happens at each stage.

📋
Step 01

Talk — We understand what you need

You share what you need — even a rough description is fine. We'll ask a few questions to understand the scope: what the part or product is, what format you need the files in, and when you need it by. A brief email or a quick phone call is enough to get started.

✏
Step 02

Plan — We map out how we'll build it

Once we understand the scope, we agree on the approach, timeline, and what the finished result will look like. We send you a written confirmation before any work starts. We also sign a confidentiality agreement (NDA — a simple document keeping your design information private) before any files are shared.

⬛
Step 03

Build — We create the design in CATIA

We build the 3D model (or assembly, drawing, or wire harness routing — whatever was agreed) in CATIA. We share a short progress update during the work so you always know where things stand. If anything unexpected comes up, we'll flag it straight away rather than waiting until the end.

📂
Step 04

Deliver — You get the finished files

You receive the finished CATIA files (and any other agreed formats, like STEP files for manufacturing or PDFs for review) organised clearly and ready to use. If anything needs adjusting after you've reviewed it, we include a revision round as standard — we don't disappear after delivery.

Data Security

Your designs stay yours.

Before any files are shared with us, we sign a confidentiality agreement (NDA). This is a standard legal document confirming we'll keep everything you share with us private. We use it with every client, every time — no exceptions.

After the project is finished and delivered, we delete your files from our systems. We never use client designs for anything other than the project they were shared for.

  • ⊙
    NDA before any file exchange

    A confidentiality agreement is signed before you share anything with us. We accept client-provided NDAs too.

  • ⊙
    Files deleted after delivery

    All your design files are deleted from our systems within 30 days of final delivery.

  • ⊙
    Secure file transfer

    We can receive and share files through your preferred secure method — encrypted email, cloud storage, or your company's own file system.
