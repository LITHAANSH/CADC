Specialized CATIA Engineering Services

Parametric Part Design

3D Solid & Complex Feature Modeling

Scope of Work: Full parametric modeling using CATIA Part Design & Generative Shape Design (GSD). We establish robust sketch geometry with 100% constraint lock, boolean operations, draft angle verification, and tooling split analysis.

Deliverables: Native .CATPart models, neutral STEP AP214/AP242 files, master parameter tables, and tree architecture documentation.

Aerospace & Automotive Structures

Primary & Secondary Airframe / BIW Structures

Scope of Work: Structural modeling of wing ribs, fuselage frames, bulkhead fittings, chassis rails, and casting/forging parts. Optimization of pocket webs, lightening cutouts, and flange stiffeners for strength-to-weight efficiency.

Deliverables: Parametric structural models compliant with AS9100 aerospace guidelines and automotive OEM manufacturing constraints.

Technical Details → Request Scope Review
⊞
Multi-Body Assemblies

Top-Down Skeleton Assemblies & DMU Kinematics

Scope of Work: Multi-part product architecture using master skeleton geometry and published datum links. Implementation of DMU Kinematics for revolute, prismatic, cylindrical, and spherical mechanism joints with dynamic clash analysis.

Deliverables: .CATProduct tree structure with verified constraint sets, sweep volume envelopes, and dynamic interference scan reports.

Enquire for Details
📐
Manufacturing Drawings

ASME Y14.5M & ISO 1101 GD&T Drawing Packs

Scope of Work: Production-ready 2D drafting generated directly from associative 3D models. Full geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T), datum reference frames, cross-sectional details, surface finishes, and automated BOM generation.

Deliverables: Fully toleranced .CATDrawing sheets, vector PDFs, DXF/DWG files, and drawing revision change histories.

Enquire for Details
⚡
Electrical Systems CAD

Electrical Harness 3D Routing & Formboard Flattening (EHI/EWR/EHF)

Scope of Work: 3D wire bundle routing in structural channels, avionics bays, and vehicle chassis. Application of bend radius rules, clamp standoff spacing, protective sleeve wrapping, and connector catalog placement. Flattening into 1:1 scale manufacturing formboards.

Deliverables: 3D harness models (EHI), bundle branch specifications, cut-length data reports, and 2D nailboard drawings.
