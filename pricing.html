Commercial FAQ

Clear, Transparent Terms

How do you structure fixed-scope quotes?

We evaluate part geometry complexity, drafting sheet counts, GD&T datum depth, and CATIA workbench requirements to provide a fixed-price proposal with clear milestone deliverables.

What is the onboarding time for surge support?

Our senior designers can review customer seed templates, execute bilateral NDAs, and begin active modeling within your CAD environment within 24 to 48 hours.

Do you support corporate team training batches?

Yes. We offer customized team cohorts tailored to your proprietary product lines, CAD standards, and schedule requirements (remote, on-site, or hybrid).

How are design revisions handled?

Every turnkey work package includes standard review cycles. Because our CAD models use clean parametric trees and published links, geometric modifications are implemented quickly without downstream tree failure.
