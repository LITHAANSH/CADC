Engagement Models

Three ways to work with us.

We don't publish day rates. We quote for scope. Below is the pricing structure — get in touch and we'll confirm a number for your specific project within 24 hours.

📋
Model 01

Project-Based

Fixed-scope delivery for a defined project. You get a quote, a timeline, and a result — without ongoing commitment.

Best For
  • One-off drawing packages
  • Single product or component launches
  • Standalone structural or MEP packages
  • Reverse engineering or DFM reviews
What's Included
  • Scoping call and written brief confirmation
  • Production by senior drafter
  • Two structured revision rounds
  • Final file archive in agreed formats
  • NDA and IP transfer included

Model 02

Overflow / Retainer

Ongoing flexible capacity, billed hourly or on a monthly block. Scale up or down in 24 hours. No minimum term after the first month.

Best For
  • Firms with fluctuating drafting load
  • Ongoing bid or construction-phase support
  • Mentorship mode alongside junior staff
  • Long-running projects with variable pace
What's Included
  • Dedicated senior drafter(s) assigned
  • Works in your templates and environment
  • Hourly or monthly block pricing
  • Weekly reporting and utilisation summary
  • Scale up/down with 24-hr notice

Model 03

Training / Academy

Per-cohort or per-seat pricing for structured upskilling. Priced on track, format, team size, and whether on-site travel is required.

Best For
  • L&D-driven team development programs
  • Transitioning 2D teams to 3D BIM
  • New hires onboarding to CAD standards
  • Pre-contract team capability uplift
What's Included
  • Custom curriculum to your standards
  • Instructor from active production team
  • Exercises and assessment materials
  • On-site, remote, or hybrid delivery
  • Post-training support for 30 days

Common Questions

Pricing, plain language.

Do you publish day rates?

No — because scope, discipline, and urgency vary too much for a day rate to be meaningful. We quote per scope and will always give you a written number with clear deliverables attached before work starts.

What's the minimum engagement size?

For project delivery, there's no formal minimum — though small individual drawings may have a minimum fee. For overflow/retainer, we typically work on a minimum 20-hour/month block to make the onboarding worthwhile for both sides.

Can I mix models?

Yes. Many clients use Project-Based delivery for one-off packages while also running an Overflow retainer for ongoing support. Academy training can run in parallel or as a standalone engagement.

How fast can you start?

For most project-based engagements: 2–5 business days from agreement to production start. Overflow retainers can be live within 24–48 hours of contract execution. Emergency turnarounds are quoted case-by-case.
