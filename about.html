About CADC

Who's behind the designs.

CADC was built by CATIA engineers who saw two problems: companies struggling to find skilled CATIA designers, and engineers struggling to learn the software properly. We solve both — through design work and through teaching.

Our Story

Started from the drawing board.

CADC started with a small team of CATIA engineers who had spent years working on aerospace and automotive design projects. They noticed the same problems everywhere: companies needed CATIA work done, but couldn't easily find people who could do it at a senior level. And engineers who wanted to learn CATIA properly had no good place to do so.

The Academy grew from that gap. If we were teaching ourselves — and each other — on the job, we could structure that into proper training programs. The same experts doing the design work became the instructors. That's still how it works today.

We're a tight team of specialists. We don't take on more work than we can do well, and we don't hand projects to people who aren't ready for them. That's what keeps the quality consistent.
