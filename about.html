About CADC

Engineering Practice & Academy

Founded by practicing CATIA design specialists, CADC provides high-precision CAD subcontracting and hands-on professional courses for aerospace, automotive, and defense engineering teams.

Our Background

Born on the CAD Engineering Floor

CADC was established by working design consultants who spent over a decade delivering flight-critical aerospace structures and automotive vehicle assemblies in CATIA V5 and 3DEXPERIENCE. We saw two recurring challenges in the industry: OEM engineering departments struggling to secure senior CAD capacity without lengthy onboarding, and junior engineers unable to find practical training that reflects real OEM drawing release standards.

We built CADC to solve both directly. On the consultancy side, our senior designers integrate seamlessly into client CAD environments, delivering clean parametric trees and ASME Y14.5 compliant drawing packs on tight milestones. On the academy side, our practicing engineers teach the exact workflows, workbenches, and geometric constraint standards we use on live industrial projects every day.

We maintain strict quality control by keeping our engineering core tight, focused, and deeply experienced in high-tolerance manufacturing.
